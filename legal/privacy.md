# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2025_

flavorHUB254 (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy.

## What Data We Collect

- Email address and username (when you sign up)
- Payment information (processed securely by third-party providers)
- Usage data (such as recipes viewed, favorites, and chat interactions)
- Device and browser information (for analytics and security)

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide and improve our services
- To process payments and manage subscriptions
- To personalize your experience
- To communicate important updates

## How We Protect Your Data

- We use secure authentication and encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Payments are processed by trusted providers (e.g. Paystack)
- We do not sell your data to third parties

## Your Rights

- You can update or delete your account at any time
- You can request access to your data by contacting us
